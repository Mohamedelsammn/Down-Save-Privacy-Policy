Privacy Policy

How Downsave handles links, media, and information on your device.

Effective date: 17 October 2026

About external processing. Submitted links are processed by our backend and an external media retrieval service. Infrastructure logging, IP-address handling, temporary caching, and that provider's retention and sharing practices have not been verified. We do not claim that these systems retain no data.

1. About Downsave

Downsave is an Android media library and management app. It lets you play and organize supported videos, audio, and images on your device, mark favorites, rename and share local files, and remove saved items. You may submit a supported media link for analysis and download. Downloading images from submitted social links is currently disabled in the reviewed production configuration.

2. Information the app handles

  • Submitted links: When you ask Downsave to analyze a link, the app sends the URL to its production backend. Depending on your selection, it also sends a video quality value or requested format such as MP3.
  • Downloaded media: The app downloads media from the URL returned by the backend and stores the resulting video or audio in app storage. It can also store a directly downloaded image where that feature is available.
  • Local library data: The app saves a local index with information such as source URL, title, file path, media type, file size, download date, thumbnail, favorites, playback history, and Gallery export status.
  • Settings: Language, preferred quality, and the Gallery auto-save preference are stored on your device.

Downsave does not require registration and the reviewed app has no account or sign-in feature. It does not collect your email address through the app; contacting support by email is your choice and takes place through your email service. The reviewed app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. Google Play in-app update and review libraries are included; their platform behavior is subject to Google's own policies.

3. How links and media are processed

Submitted URLs are sent over HTTPS to Downsave's production backend at downhub.arabplus4tech.com/resolve to identify available media. The owner confirms that the backend processes links using a third-party media retrieval service, and the submitted URL is provided to that service as part of retrieval. Source platforms and media delivery hosts may also receive information needed to fulfill the request. The app then retrieves media from the returned download URL. Their data practices have not been independently verified.

Storage and logging status: The owner reports that submitted media URLs are not intentionally stored in an application database and that submitted media is not intentionally stored on the server. Temporary processing or caching may still occur. We have not verified whether infrastructure systems keep access or error logs, whether IP addresses or request metadata are retained, or what the external provider keeps or shares. We therefore cannot state a retention period for those systems. We will update this policy when these practices are confirmed.

4. On-device storage and Gallery

Downloaded files, library records, favorites, and app settings are stored on your device. The Gallery auto-save setting is enabled by default and can be changed in Settings. When enabled, supported videos are also saved to the device Gallery after download; images can be saved to Gallery through the image viewer. A Gallery copy is stored in shared device media storage and may remain after the in-app copy is removed.

5. Android permissions

Downsave uses internet access to analyze links and download media. On Android 7–10, Gallery saving may request storage write access. Newer Android versions use the system media interface for Gallery saving without broad media-read permission. The app does not request broad photo, video, or audio read access in the reviewed release. If you choose Set as Ringtone, Android may ask you to allow modification of system settings.

6. Third-party services

Links and media may originate from third-party platforms, and media downloads may connect to their services or delivery hosts. Downsave is independent of those platforms. Their terms and privacy practices apply to their services. Google Play services may support in-app updates and review prompts on compatible devices.

7. Security

The app submits URLs to its configured backend over HTTPS and accepts HTTPS media download URLs. On-device files are subject to Android's storage controls. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. The backend's operational security controls have not been independently verified.

8. Retention and deletion

Downloaded files and library entries remain on your device until you delete an item, use Clear Downloads, uninstall the app, or the device removes app data. The app's Delete and Clear Downloads controls attempt to remove the relevant in-app files and clear their library entries; a file operation can fail, so check device storage if an item remains. Gallery copies may need to be removed separately from your device Gallery. The owner reports no intentional application-database storage of submitted URLs or server-side storage of submitted media, but temporary caches, infrastructure logs, and third-party retention remain unverified. We cannot currently promise a server-side deletion period or process.

9. Children's privacy

Downsave is not designed for children. We do not knowingly provide a child-directed account or social feature. If you believe a child has submitted information through the app, contact us so we can investigate the request in light of the backend's confirmed data practices.

10. Your choices and rights

You can choose which links to submit, disable Gallery auto-save, delete in-app downloads, remove Gallery copies, and uninstall Downsave. Depending on your location, you may have additional legal rights relating to personal information. Email us to make a privacy inquiry or rights request. We will investigate based on the information available and the applicable law; any server-side deletion process still needs confirmation.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will revise the effective date when material changes are published and, where required, communicate them through the app or other appropriate channels.
