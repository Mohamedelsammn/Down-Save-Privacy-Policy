Privacy Policy

How Downsave handles personal media, device access, and app settings.

Effective date pending verification and publication

Draft pending implementation review. The only Flutter source available in this workspace is the previous downloader build. Device import, editing, storage, permissions, Clear Data, and network behavior must be checked against the redesigned source before this policy is published.

1. About Downsave

Downsave is described by its owner as a personal media manager, player, and editor for videos, images, and audio. Users can import media from their device, view or play supported files, organize favorites, rename and share files, edit supported media, and set supported audio as ringtones. Exact editing tools and data flows require verification against the current app build.

2. Media and device access

Importing personal media requires access to files selected or made available on the device. The Android permissions and selection methods used by the redesigned app have not yet been verified. The final policy will identify which media permissions are requested, when they are used, and whether access is limited to selected files.

3. Local media processing and storage

The redesigned app is intended to process imported media and keep its library data on the device. The current source is needed to confirm where originals, edited results, thumbnails, favorites, and settings are stored, whether any copies are created, and whether any media or metadata leaves the device. No absolute no-collection or no-sharing statement is made in this draft.

4. Playback, editing, and organization

Downsave uses selected media for playback, image viewing, editing, organization, favorites, and renaming. The exact supported editing operations and whether edits replace originals or create new files must be verified before publication.

5. Sharing and ringtone actions

When you choose to share a file, Android may pass it to the app or service you select, which then handles it under its own terms and privacy practices. Setting an audio file as a ringtone may create a system media entry or require permission to change Android system settings. The exact behavior of the redesigned app remains to be verified.

6. Deletion and Clear Data

The owner describes file deletion and a Clear Data control in Settings. The current implementation must be checked to explain precisely which in-app files, library records, edited outputs, and shared or system copies these actions remove. Copies shared with other apps may need separate deletion.

7. Accounts, network services, and security

The previous app did not require registration, but the redesigned build must be checked before this policy makes a current account claim. Network calls and integrated SDKs also require review. Android storage protections apply to local app data; the final policy will describe verified security practices without promising absolute protection.

8. Children's privacy

Downsave is not described as a child-directed app. The current app and any integrated services must be reviewed before making detailed statements about children's data. Parents or guardians can contact us with concerns.

9. Your choices and rights

You can decide which personal media to use with the app and can use available local deletion controls. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding personal information. Email us with privacy questions or requests.

10. Changes to this policy

We will publish an effective date when the policy has been verified against the redesigned app. Future material changes will be reflected here and communicated where required.
