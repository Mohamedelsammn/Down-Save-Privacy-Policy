Privacy Policy

How Downsave handles links, media, and information on your device.

Draft prepared 17 October 2026 · Effective date pending publication

Draft — backend details pending. The production server's request logging, IP handling, retention, and deletion practices have not been confirmed. This policy is not final and must not be used as the app's published Google Play privacy policy yet.

1. About Downsave

Downsave is an Android app that helps users save, access, play, and organize supported media. It can analyze a media link you submit, download supported video or audio, and manage downloaded files in an on-device library. The app also displays local images. Downloading images from submitted social links is currently disabled in the production configuration.

2. Information the app handles

  • Submitted links: When you ask Downsave to analyze a link, the app sends the URL to its production backend. Depending on your selection, it also sends a video quality value or requested format such as MP3.
  • Downloaded media: The app downloads media from the URL returned by the backend and stores the resulting video or audio in app storage. It can also store a directly downloaded image where that feature is available.
  • Local library data: The app saves a local index with information such as source URL, title, file path, media type, file size, download date, thumbnail, favorites, playback history, and Gallery export status.
  • Settings: Language, preferred quality, and the Gallery auto-save preference are stored on your device.

The reviewed app has no account or sign-in feature and does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. Google Play in-app update and review libraries are included; their platform behavior is subject to Google's own policies.

3. How links and media are processed

Submitted URLs are sent over HTTPS to Downsave's production backend at downhub.arabplus4tech.com/resolve to identify available media. The backend may contact the source platform to resolve the link. The app then retrieves media from the returned download URL. Those platforms and delivery hosts may receive information needed for the request under their own privacy practices.

Unverified server practices: We have not confirmed whether the backend or its hosting provider logs submitted URLs, IP addresses, request metadata, errors, or other information; how long any such records are kept; or how they can be deleted. These points require confirmation before final publication and before completing Google Play Data Safety answers.

4. On-device storage and Gallery

Downloaded files, library records, favorites, and app settings are stored on your device. The Gallery auto-save setting is enabled by default and can be changed in Settings. When enabled, supported videos are also saved to the device Gallery after download; images can be saved to Gallery through the image viewer. A Gallery copy is stored in shared device media storage and may remain after the in-app copy is removed.

5. Android permissions

Downsave uses internet access to analyze links and download media. On Android 7–10, Gallery saving may request storage write access. Newer Android versions use the system media interface for Gallery saving without broad media-read permission. The app does not request broad photo, video, or audio read access in the reviewed release. If you choose Set as Ringtone, Android may ask you to allow modification of system settings.

6. Third-party services

Links and media may originate from third-party platforms, and media downloads may connect to their services or delivery hosts. Downsave is independent of those platforms. Their terms and privacy practices apply to their services. Google Play services may support in-app updates and review prompts on compatible devices.

7. Security

The app submits URLs to its configured backend over HTTPS and accepts HTTPS media download URLs. On-device files are subject to Android's storage controls. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. The backend's operational security controls have not been independently verified for this draft.

8. Retention and deletion

Local files and library entries remain on your device until you delete them, clear downloads, uninstall the app, or the device removes app data. You can remove in-app downloads and change settings in the app. Gallery copies may need to be removed separately from your device Gallery. Backend and hosting-provider retention and deletion are still unverified; we cannot currently promise a server-side deletion period or process.

9. Children's privacy

Downsave is not designed for children. We do not knowingly provide a child-directed account or social feature. If you believe a child has submitted information through the app, contact us so we can investigate the request in light of the backend's confirmed data practices.

10. Your choices and rights

You can choose which links to submit, disable Gallery auto-save, delete in-app downloads, remove Gallery copies, and uninstall Downsave. Depending on your location, you may have additional legal rights relating to personal information. Email us to make a privacy inquiry or rights request. We will need to confirm the backend's data handling before describing any server-side request process or outcome.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The published version will show an effective date. Material changes will be reflected here and, where required, communicated through the app or other appropriate channels.
